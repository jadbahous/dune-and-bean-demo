About

Dune & Bean is a small-batch roastery born out of a desire to slow Doha's mornings down

Dune & Bean

We have a taste for slow mornings and a deep respect for the bean

How It Started

A Shared Love Of Good Coffee

Dune & Bean started with a simple frustration: it was hard to find a truly good cup in Al Sadd. Not instant, not over-sweetened, not an afterthought — just coffee that had been sourced with care and roasted with attention.

So we started small. A handful of green bean origins, a secondhand roaster, and a lot of trial and error. What began as batches shared with neighbors has grown into a proper roastery and café, though the instinct to keep things small and considered hasn't changed.

The Space

A Home In Al Sadd

We wanted a room people would actually want to sit in — wood tables worn in by regulars, big windows for the morning light, and enough space between seats that a conversation (or a laptop, or a book) doesn't feel crowded.

It's a space built for lingering rather than turning tables. Come for a quick espresso at the counter, or stay for the whole afternoon — either way, you're welcome here.

What Guides Us

Three Things We Won't Compromise On

Sourcing

We buy direct where we can and pay for quality, not volume — every origin is chosen, not just available.

Roasting

Small batches, roasted weekly and cupped constantly — we'd rather run out than serve a stale bag.

Community

This is a neighborhood room first — long tables, regulars by name, and space to actually sit down.
